Oh, Washington! A place with astounding history,
Oh, Washington! A place with beauty and diversity.

Just look at those wild, green trees!
The most fascinating sight I see.
Evergreen branches that touch the sky,
Where mossy giants stand thick and high.

Lots and lots of trees that stand in the Yakima sun,
Red, green, and yellow sprout when the day is done.
Crispy and sweet with a juice that flows,
The finest apples Washington grows.

Summer waves splash on the shore,
As tulip fields grow and autumn rain clouds roar.
Winter snow and summer skies blue,
All these seasons have wonderful views.

The wind soars through the Hemlock leaves,
In the peaceful magic nature weaves.
A heavenly hike through the land,
With Washington's beauty on every hand.

Towering high in the cloudy sky,
Where the silver eagles fly.
High and tranquil, it stands so grand,
This is the mountain, of our beautiful land.

From the mountains to the sea, this is the place for me!
Wonderful Washington, amazing and grand,
Is the best state in all of the land.
